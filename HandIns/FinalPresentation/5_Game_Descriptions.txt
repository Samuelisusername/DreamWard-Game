Name
==================
Dreamward

20 Words
==================
A card-based tower defense game where you protect yourself and your flock from nightmares, building defenses each day to survive the night.

100 Words
==================
Dreamward is a single-player isometric tower defense game set in a forest where nightmares roam the night. 
Each day, you prepare your defenses using a hand of cards. Place towers, walls, and conjured structures strategically across the map. 
Protect your flock, whose peaceful presence generates the sanity you need to survive. 
When night falls, waves of nightmares advance to test your strategy. 
Manage your resources wisely, adapt your strategy round by round, and discover new cards by herding your sheep across farther meadows. 
Survive until dawn and face an ever growing threat with each passing night.
