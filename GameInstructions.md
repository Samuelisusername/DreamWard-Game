# Dreamward

Number of players: Singleplayer

Input method: Mouse & Keyboard

## Controls
The game starts at day time. 
Sheep instruct the player to defend them.

The player can buy and build cards by dragging them into the map. Cards can be shown and hidden by pressing C.
Hovering over placed tiles gives further information on them.

Most placed tiles can be destroyed or moved by selecting the corresponding button to the left.
Clicking and holding on a meadow moves sheep. This can reveal new cards for later nights and yield more resources.

Having made appropriate preparations, the player can choose to start the night by holding the spacebar.
Night can be sped up by pressing S. Monsters can be clicked to set them as a priority target.
If the defenses are successful, the monsters are defeated and the player has sanity left, daytime starts anew.

Gamepad is not supported.

| Action | Keyboard | 
| ------ | -------- | 
|start night| spacebar | 
|hide cards | C | 
|speed up | S | 
|pause  | P or esc |
